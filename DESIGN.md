# Visual Design System: Minimalist Ledger

## Thesis
A stripped-back, high-contrast digital notebook that puts data first.

## Visual Rules

### Palette & Materials
- **Background**: Off-white paper (`#fcfcfc`)
- **Text & Primary Ink**: True black (`#111111`)
- **Accents**: Sparse gray (`#a0a0a0`) and alert red (`#ea4335`)
- **Materials**: Clean typography and hairline rules. No shadows, no gradients, no frosted glass.

### Type & Composition
- **Layout**: Clean black text on an off-white background, separated by thin, precise hairline rules.
- **Typography**: Strictly utilitarian but elegant. Content is strictly aligned to a structural grid.

### Topology & Navigation
- Utilitarian digital notebook layout.
- The interface feels like a printed receipt or physical ledger, stacking chronologically with high contrast.
